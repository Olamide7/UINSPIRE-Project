Life at UINSPIRE

Why you'll love it here

Remote-First Culture

Work from anywhere. We care about what you build, not where you sit. We provide a $2,000 home office stipend.

Full Health Coverage

100% covered premium health, dental, and vision insurance for you and 80% for your dependents.

Flexible Time Off

Take the time you need to recharge. We enforce a minimum of 20 days off per year because burnout isn't a badge of honor.

Learning Budget

$1,500 annual budget for courses, books, and conferences. If it makes you better at your job, we'll pay for it.

Parental Leave

16 weeks of fully paid leave for all new parents, plus a flexible return-to-work schedule.

Profit Sharing

When the company wins, everyone wins. We distribute 10% of annual profits back to the team.
