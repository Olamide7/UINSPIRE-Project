Our Mission

Making technology work for people, not the other way around

Too many organizations are held back by technology that was supposed to help them move forward. We exist to change that. Every project we take on starts with a simple question: what does your business actually need?

We don't push platforms, sell licenses, or recommend solutions that require a decade-long commitment. We build what works, hand over the keys, and make sure your team can run it without us.

Our Vision

A world where every organization — regardless of size or industry — has access to technology that genuinely serves its people and purpose.

Our Journey

A decade of building
things that last

2014

The Beginning

Founded in a shared office with three people and a conviction that consulting could be done differently. First client: a mid-size logistics company that needed a warehouse management system.

2016

First Major Enterprise Client

Won our first enterprise engagement with Meridian Financial. The project was supposed to last 6 months — we're still their technology partner today.

2018

Expanding the Team

Grew to 30 people. Opened our second office and launched our cloud practice. Completed our 50th project.

2020

Adapting & Growing

Shifted to remote-first operations. Helped 15 clients rapidly digitize their operations during a period when it mattered most. Launched our data analytics practice.

2022

Global Reach

Expanded to three continents. Crossed the 200-project milestone. Recognized as a Top 50 technology consultancy by Enterprise Tech Review.

Today

Looking Forward

80+ team members, 240+ projects delivered, and a client retention rate we're genuinely proud of. Still asking the same question we started with: what does your business actually need?
